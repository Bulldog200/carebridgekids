清晰可信的儿童健康信息
Clear, Trusted Pediatric Health Info

康桥儿童健康是一个中英双语平台，帮助家长和照护者——尤其是移民家庭——便捷获取来自CDC等权威机构的儿童健康资源。

A bilingual platform helping parents and caregivers, especially immigrant families, access trusted children's health resources from the CDC and other official sources.

为什么选择康桥儿童健康？

Why CareBridge Kids?

许多家庭在获取关键儿童健康信息时面临语言和文化的隔阂。 康桥儿童健康系统整理并链接来自美国CDC、AAP及纽约市/州卫生部门的官方信息，用中英双语呈现，让家长无需在多个网站间辗转查找。

Many families face language barriers when accessing pediatric health information. We organize official guidance from the CDC, AAP, and NYC/NYS health departments into one bilingual, easy-to-navigate resource.

了解我们的使命 Learn about our mission

本网站所有内容仅用于教育目的，不构成医疗建议。所有信息均直接链接至官方权威来源，确保准确与可信。

Everything here is for educational purposes only and links directly back to official sources — so you always know the information is accurate and current.
