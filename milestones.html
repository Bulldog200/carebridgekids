儿童发展里程碑查询 | Developmental Milestones Checker

输入孩子的出生日期，查看美国疾病控制与预防中心（CDC）官方的年龄发展里程碑清单 | Enter your child's date of birth to view the official CDC developmental milestone checklist for their age

本工具仅根据孩子的出生日期计算年龄，并直接链接到美国疾病控制与预防中心（CDC）"Learn the Signs. Act Early." 项目的官方里程碑清单，包括CDC官方中文版PDF清单。本网站不编写或修改任何医学内容，所有清单均为CDC官方原文。

This tool calculates your child's age from their date of birth and links directly to the official milestone checklists from the CDC's "Learn the Signs. Act Early." program, including the CDC's official Chinese-language PDF checklist. This website does not write or alter any medical content; every checklist shown is the CDC's own official document.


查看中文清单（CDC官方）View Chinese Checklist (CDC) → English Checklist（英文版）→

本工具不提供医疗诊断或建议，仅指引到CDC官方发展里程碑资源。每个孩子的发展速度不同；如果您对孩子的发展有任何担忧，请务必咨询儿科医生，或联系您所在州的儿童早期干预项目。

This tool does not provide medical diagnosis or advice. It only directs you to the CDC's official developmental milestone resources. Every child develops at their own pace. If you have any concerns about your child's development, please talk to your pediatrician or contact your state's Early Intervention program.
